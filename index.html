What we teach

Skills that make a difference in everyday work.

Programmes can be tailored to the learning needs of students, educators and corporate teams.

Communication foundations

  • Understanding and barriers to communication
  • Listening skills and giving feedback
  • Non-verbal communication
  • Giving, receiving and following instructions
  • Questioning skills and paraphrasing

Presence & etiquette

  • Business etiquette and professional presentation
  • Telephone etiquette
  • Dining etiquette and table manners
  • Adapting across personality, culture and gender

Assertiveness & leadership

  • Assertive versus aggressive communication
  • Leadership, motivation and feedback
  • Time, stress and team management
  • Conflict resolution and interpersonal skills

Corporate & customer skills

  • Communication in customer service
  • Handling difficult conversations
  • Oral communication and meetings
  • Campus-to-corporate transition and accountability
